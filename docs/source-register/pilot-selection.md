# Pilot District Selection: Khordha District, Odisha

## Decision Summary
- **Selected District:** Khordha (Census 2011 Code: 386; ISO 3166-2: IN-OD-29 / IN-OD-KH)
- **State:** Odisha (ISO: IN-OD)
- **Selection Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** APPROVED for Pilot (Phase 0)

## Evaluation Against Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement | Khordha Assessment | Status |
|-----------|-------------|--------------------|--------|
| **Boundary Coverage** | District, Block, and Village boundaries available | Khordha includes 10 CD Blocks and Bhubaneswar Municipal Corporation. Open boundaries exist via Survey of India and Datameet spatial repositories. | VERIFIED |
| **Population Data** | Census tables with total, rural/urban, literacy, sex ratio | Full 2011 Census Primary Census Abstract (PCA) available for Khordha and constituent subdivisions down to village level. | VERIFIED |
| **Health Records** | Official health bulletins, IDSP/NCDC public records, or facility registers | NHM Odisha and MoHFW publish district-level health statistics, facility listings (AIIMS Bhubaneswar, Capital Hospital, CHCs/PHCs). | VERIFIED |
| **Place Search** | Geocoding and reverse geocoding via Nominatim/OSM | Nominatim returns high-confidence bounding polygons and point centroids for Khordha, Bhubaneswar, Jatni, Khordha town, and rural tehsils. | VERIFIED |

## Justification
Khordha provides an ideal balance of urban agglomeration (Bhubaneswar, the state capital) and extensive rural subdistricts (Begunia, Banapur, Bolagarh, Chilika, Tangi). This offers a rigorous test for resolving multi-level administrative hierarchies, handling varying degrees of data completeness, and demonstrating offline resilience without relying on fictional or interpolated records.
