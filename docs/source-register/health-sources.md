# Health Data Source Register

## Source 1: Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MoHFW) / NHM Odisha
- **Publisher:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Govt. of India / National Health Mission Odisha
- **URL:** https://mohfw.gov.in / https://nrhmorissa.gov.in
- **Licence/Terms:** Open Government Data License - India
- **Attribution Required:** "Source: Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India"
- **Access Mode:** download / manual
- **Format:** PDF bulletins, CSV data tables
- **Coverage:** District level (Khordha)
- **Vintage/Version:** Periodic publications (monthly/quarterly)
- **Status:** VERIFIED WITH LIMITS
- **Notes:** Public bulletins provide aggregate facility statistics and surveillance alerts. Ingestion requires structured PDF extraction and verification check.
